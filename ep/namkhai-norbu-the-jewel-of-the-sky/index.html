Episode 90

Namkhai Norbu: The Jewel of the Sky

25 October 2026 · 1:00:16

An eight-chapter guided course on Chogyal Namkhai Norbu (1938-2018): the recognized tulku who never took a throne, the country doctor of Khamdogar who gave him direct introduction, the Chengdu years and the dream that turned him home, the collapse of Tibet and the road through Sikkim, the Gangtok textbook years and Tucci's invitation, thirty years as professor of Tibetan at Naples while living-room talks grew into a worldwide community, the view he taught (direct introduction, no doubt remaining, integration; the mirror and its infinite potentiality; self-liberation beside renunciation and transformation), the gars built like nomads' camps with no hierarchy, Santi Maha Sangha and the Longsal terma received in clear light dreams, the honors of his last decade, and what he left in writing for readers with and without transmission. An independent critical listening course: every claim is labeled to its source, with biographical reference, open-access scholarship, the community's own published transcripts and publishing catalog, and his public lecture recordings kept on separate shelves throughout. Recognition, realization, dream and terma claims are reported as the tradition's own claims, never asserted as fact; there are no practice instructions anywhere in this course, and the community's own transmission-registration rule is quoted and honored as the boundary. Quotations are short and attributed; his books are presumed in copyright and are described by title and published blurb only; this release grants no rights in any underlying work. Lecture quotations are transcribed from YouTube's machine-generated captions (archived capture; the live track may differ). Narrated by Tara, an AI voice.
