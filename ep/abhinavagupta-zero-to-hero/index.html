22 October 2026 · 1:03:36

An own-words introduction to Abhinavagupta as a thinker: Kashmir, consciousness and freedom, recognition, the means, aesthetics and rasa, and later reception. Narrated by Tara, an AI voice. Provisional interpretation, not an authoritative translation, lineage transmission or practice authorization. Ritual and Kaula material is overview only. Traditional disappearance and revelation stories are labeled as legends. Comparisons with Vedanta, Buddhism and Kant are interpretive; claims about borrowing and rasa remain scholarly debates. Reference caveats: the chapter-1 sketch of earlier Kashmir thinkers compresses distinct figures and periods and should not be read as a precise chronology. Shanta-rasa's origins and Anandavardhana's role are debated; Abhinavagupta's defence should not be treated as proof of who first proposed it. The Susan Feagin mention is unsourced in this script and should not be relied on as a specific scholarly attribution.
