# The Buddha's Nine Progressive Attainments
Released 3 November 2026. Episode102. Narrated by Tara, an AI voice; narration produced with Muse. 17:58.557, eleven chapters.

## Source and method
A textual study of AN9.33, Anupubbaviharasamapatti Sutta. The episode follows the discourse's nine successive abidings and attainments through the factors said to cease. New provisional working English renderings from the Pali are interleaved with original commentary. These are not certified scholarly translations, the historical Buddha's recorded voice, or a substitute for a teacher.

Primary Pali witness: Mahasangiti text presented by SuttaCentral. An independent Pali witness adapted from the Sri Lanka Buddha Jayanti Tipitaka supplies the opening setting and exhortation where the primary transcription elides them. For the second, third and fourth jhana formulas, the primary witness abbreviates; full standard formulas are supplied and checked against DN2 and the independent AN witness. This expansion is disclosed in the audio. DN2's later destruction-of-taints passage is used for comparison, not imported into AN9.33.

The qualifier tadangena is rendered "in that respect". Repeated cessation within an attainment is not claimed to be permanent eradication. The ninth attainment is not presented as automatically certifying arahantship. This is textual explanation, not guided induction of cessation or attainment certification. No restricted-practice procedures are added.

## Translation comparison and rights
Production review compared interpretive choices with published translations by Bhikkhu Sujato and Thanissaro Bhikkhu.
