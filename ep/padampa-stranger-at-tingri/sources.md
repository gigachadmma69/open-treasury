## Attribution and uncertainty
Three, five and seven Tibet visits are preserved as differing accounts, not harmonized into invented dates. The familiar Hundred's attribution and recension problems are attributed to Dan Martin's argument and its date, not a timeless proof of absence. Himalayan Art Resources and Treasury of Lives reproduce the same authored biography, not independent corroboration. Ani Ngawang is a later custodian, not a contemporary witness to Padampa. No full-read claim is made for the early Zhije collection, Blue Annals, Lion of Siddhas or entire dissertation.
