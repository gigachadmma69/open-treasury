# The Self Is the Dancer
## Kashmir Shaivism on the Play of the World

An original eleven-chapter comparative course. Macbeth's speech belongs to a dramatic character at catastrophe, not a declaration of Shakespeare's own philosophy. The Shaiva texts are not made to agree with Macbeth, Vedanta, or one another. Our spectator-seat synthesis and listening illustration are labeled readings, not initiation or a promised realization method. Stop an optional illustration if it feels distressing or unreal. Metaphysical self-limitation is not human consent to harm. No doctrine overrides a present, revocable no.
