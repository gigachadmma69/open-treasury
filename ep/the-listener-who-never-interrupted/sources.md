## Quotation and claim map

### Chapter 1 - exemplary listener

The opening thought experiment is original. It is not a real person/contact story. 'What we wish becomes evidence' is the course's interpretive question, not Hoffmann's directly stated doctrine.

### Chapter 2 - Clara and coffee

Approximately lines 5670-5780. Narrator describes Clara sympathetically, not very talkative but discriminating; some call her cold, others value her. Nathanael reads during breakfast preparation; she explains coffee will boil over if she gazes at him as requested; he shuts his book and withdraws angrily. Narrator calls his later writing exceedingly tedious. These are paraphrases of inspected scenes, not invented domestic biography of Hoffmann.

The course explicitly says this is not clinical evidence, does not equate psychological distress with vanity or violence, and makes the attention/household-task connection as our reading. A sufferer's distress does not establish an entitlement to someone else's attention.

### Chapter 3 - optical glass

Approximately lines 5890-5995 and 6025-6050. Before the purchase, Nathanael notices Olimpia's stillness without abandoning Clara. Coppola supplies a small optical glass; looking through it intensifies the interpreted life/love in the gaze. He uses it at concert. Script says 'he reads' to preserve focalization rather than assert Olimpia's loving intention as a fact. The instrument/desire interaction is expressly interpretation.

No specific universal claim about technology, optics, perception or modern platforms is made.

### Chapter 4 - timing

Approximately lines 6050-6075 and 6125-6160. Nathanael finds her timing so exact it exposes his own errors. Siegmund describes her music/dance as machine-like and disturbing. The course compares their interpretations, not treating movement or timing as a genuine humanity test. No assertion that real machines never err or humans cannot keep time.

### Chapter 7 - Poe's weak proof

Our criticism is logical, not an unsourced empirical assertion about a particular current system.
