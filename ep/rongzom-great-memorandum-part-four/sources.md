# Reading Rongzom, Part Four: source credits

Great Memorandum on the View (lta ba'i brjed byang chen mo) by Rongzom Chokyi Zangpo. The short passages in this episode are The Open Treasury's own study renderings from the Tibetan: AI work offered for scholar review, not a definitive translation, and not a paraphrase of any existing English translation.

Working witness: Leh gsung thor bu print, Buddhist Digital Resource Center MW30125, image volume I5886, printed page 203 (scan 215), with a few words of printed page 204 (scan 216).
https://library.bdrc.io/show/bdr:MW30125_DC9241

Comparison witness (weak, used only as a guide): modern typeset e-text, BDRC W29621, through OpenPecha I47678AE1.
https://github.com/Openpecha-Data/I47678AE1

Locus and variant record (abridged). Printed p.203 verso: the aggregates sentence ends "phung po lnga tsar rdzas su gcig go zhes 'dod do" in the Leh print; the second witness has a longer form, not used. Line 3 opens "shing rta dang / gdan" (a chariot and a seat); the rest of that line is unread. Line 4: "'byung ba che bo bzhi dang / mkha' dang" ... "gnyis ste dgu rdzas rang gi ngo bos 'dod do", the middle members unread. Printed p.204: only the word "rung" (fit to be held) was confirmed in the etymology lines; the etymology idea is given as cited by Dominic Sur (2015 dissertation, University of Virginia, note 245, citing the modern edition RZSB 2.09.09-11) and is not our reading.

Correction to Part Three: the count of twenty-one substances is not settled word for word in either witness we can read, and should be treated as unconfirmed. See the correction note on the Part Three page.
