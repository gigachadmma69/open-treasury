What Counts as a Substance: Reading Rongzom, Part Four

A short, careful episode on how Rongzom Chokyi Zangpo's Great Memorandum on the View treats the word substance (rdzas, Sanskrit dravya): what the printed page lets us read about the five aggregates as one substance, examples such as a chariot and a seat, and a list of nine substances held to exist by their own nature. It also carries an explicit correction of Part Three about the count of twenty-one substances, and the worldly etymology of the word as cited in scholarship.
