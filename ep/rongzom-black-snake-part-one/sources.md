## Reading limits

The episode reads the complete Black Snake Discourse in the translation credited above, with separately announced original commentary. Footnote numbers, punctuation and typographic details are adjusted for speech. The translator's footnotes and bibliography remain available at the source.

This is a study reading, not a critical edition, a new Tibetan translation, an empowerment or lineage transmission. It is Part One of a larger corpus project, not a claim to have covered the whole extant corpus. The course has not been reviewed by an expert Tibetanist. Rongzom's polemical hierarchy is identified as an argument, not treated as a neutral survey of other Buddhist schools.
