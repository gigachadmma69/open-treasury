Namkha Che: Space, Seeking, and the Limits of Making

31 October 2026 | 46:15 | 22 chapters

An interpretive companion following the fifty-five-verse sequence of Dorje Sempa Namkha Che: space, compassion, non-making, seeking, and the limits of names. Published translations and textual variants are compared without pretending that disputed lines have been settled.

Original commentary, not a certified translation of all fifty-five verses, not a reading of a modern translation, and not the separate full verse-by-verse study course. The traditional Garab Dorje attribution is distinguished from historical scholarship. No restricted practice instructions or claims of attainment.

Sources and further reading

  • Christopher Wilkinson, short text and critical Tibetan, RET 24 (2012), pp. 21-80
  • Adriano Clemente, longer-tantra correspondences, RET 61 (2021), pp. 184-208
  • Keith Dowman, five selected passages in the Dochu order
