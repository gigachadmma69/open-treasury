# Namkha Che: Space, Seeking, and the Limits of Making

31 October 2026. Interpretive companion, 46:15, 22 chapters.

An interpretive companion following the fifty-five-verse sequence of Dorje Sempa Namkha Che: space, compassion, non-making, seeking, and the limits of names. Published translations and textual variants are compared without pretending that disputed lines have been settled.

Original commentary, not a certified translation of all fifty-five verses, not a reading of a modern translation, and not the separate full verse-by-verse study course. The traditional Garab Dorje attribution is distinguished from historical scholarship. No restricted practice instructions or claims of attainment.

- Dylan Esler, RET 24, pp. 95-96 and notes 80-87: targeted comparison on the doctor analogy and non-quietism, in the same RET 24 issue linked above. This was a targeted passage, not a full reading of Esler's article.

Wilkinson and Clemente were read in full. Clemente's English passages concern corresponding sections of the longer thirty-three-chapter tantra, not an identical witness of every short-text line. Dowman's five passages correspond to verses 29, 30, 1, 41 and 46 in the arrangement followed here. Calverley's full translation and the 2016 Iron Commentary were not read or used as comparison evidence.

## Interpretation and open questions

Alternative readings remain open for particles, word order, pronouns and witness variants, especially in verses 34, 42-45, 47, 49-51 and 55. Our preferred thematic readings are interpretations, not proof of a single grammatical resolution or of an author's intention. The companion distinguishes traditional birth and age-seven recitation accounts rather than presenting either as modern biography.

The program discusses published scholarship and uses original explanation with attributed short phrases. It does not reproduce a modern translation or treat public availability of a source as a podcast licence. No purchased or restricted text is narrated.
