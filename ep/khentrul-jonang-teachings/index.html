Episode 85

Khentrul Rinpoche: The Jonang Teachings

25 October 2026 · 1:12:45

Nine episodes, one course: love as enjoyment and compassion as fearlessness, death as daily practice, zhentong ('empty of other'), the Kalachakra and its Six Vajra Yogas at map level, Shambhala and the Golden Age, and how the Jonang school survived three centuries of being declared dead. An independent critical listening course on the public teachings of Shar Khentrul Jamphel Lodro Rinpoche: short attributed quotes, examined critically, including where the tradition's claims outrun verification. Commentary, not endorsement; a listening course is not a background check. Nothing here is practice instruction - completion-stage material appears as names, aims and claims only. Narrated by Tara, an AI voice, with a special appearance by guest narrator Muse in one section of episode 1, volume matched.
