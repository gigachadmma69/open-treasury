# David Germano: History, Experience, and the Work of Contemplation

Original study course about selected scholarship and collaborations. Not taught, commissioned or authorized by David Germano. Text researched31October2026. Narration64:13, six parts.

## What this course covers

The history of the Great Perfection through Germano's provisional pristine/funerary analysis; alaya, continuity and embodiment in a chapter coauthored with William S.Waldron; effort and agency in his2025 framework; contemporary poetry translated with Tashi Dekyid Monet; digital knowledge communities; and education coauthored with Aneel Chima. The closing questions and cross-reading synthesis are our own.

## Coverage limits

The1994Architecture and Absence article and1997Princeton chapters were not fully read for this production. The2007Buddhist studies/area studies/humanities computing article was located at abstract level only. These omissions are acknowledged in the audio. The course is selective, not an exhaustive tour of Germano's output. His listed Treasury of Words and Meanings translation is not described as a completed published book.

## How to interpret the claims

Historical chronologies and movement relationships are attributed proposals, not independent verification of every primary Tibetan witness. The chapter's impure ground is not indiscriminately equated with rigpa or every other use of ground. The2025thirteen-pair framework is provisional, not a validated attainment scale; its microphenomenological program is prospective, not completed results. Miraculous, visionary and after-death accounts are not presented as established scientific findings. No advanced practice procedures are supplied.
