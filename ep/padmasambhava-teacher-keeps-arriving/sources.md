# Padmasambhava: The Teacher Who Keeps Arriving
Released 6 November 2026. 18 chapters, 8,092 spoken words, 60 minutes 39 seconds.

A bounded original course: traditional narrative and textual comparison, not independently verified modern biography, a complete corpus account or a practice transmission.
