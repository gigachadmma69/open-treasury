Padmasambhava: The Teacher Who Keeps Arriving

6 November 2026 | 60:39 | Eighteen chapters

A source-stacked course on Padmasambhava: early manuscript traces, the different biographies of Kongtrul and Taranatha, women as teachers and transmitters, the founding of Samye, and the relation of view and conduct.

Traditional narrative and textual comparison, not independently verified modern biography or a complete corpus account. Nonprocedural study, not empowerment or practice transmission.
