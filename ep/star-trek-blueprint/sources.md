## What this is
Original course commentary arguing that Star Trek's Federation is the closest shared cultural blueprint for a post-scarcity, libertarian-socialist society with a contemplative (yogi) core, and stress-testing that claim against the franchise's own counterpoints (Ferengi, Section 31, the Maquis, the Bell Riots). Opinion is labeled as opinion in the narration; facts are called out as facts.
