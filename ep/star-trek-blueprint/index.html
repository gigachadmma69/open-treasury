The Blueprint: Star Trek and the Post-Scarcity Yogi Dream

14 November 2026 | 39:26 | Nine chapters

An original critical course interpreting Star Trek's Federation as a post-scarcity, libertarian-socialist blueprint with a contemplative core. The argument is an interpretation, tested against the franchise's own counterpoints: Ferengi, Section 31, the Maquis and the Bell Riots.

The course separates dialogue, fictional events and our reading. It does not claim the franchise presents one consistent economic chronology or that its political and spiritual themes have only one meaning.
