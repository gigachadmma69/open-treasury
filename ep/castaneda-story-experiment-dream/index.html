Castaneda: The Story, the Experiment, the Dream

6 November 2026 | 38:25 | Twelve chapters

A critical course with a practical heart: historical doubts about Castaneda, selected first-gate dreaming material, lucid-dream research and a James-inspired framework for examining experience without surrendering judgment.

Selected first-gate study of the 1993 HarperCollins edition, printed pages 21–27 and 30–31, not an all-gates or definitive course. Original critical commentary and paraphrase, not a reading of the book. No sleep-deprivation, substance or stimulation protocol.
