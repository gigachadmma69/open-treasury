One Vehicle, One Taste: The One Vehicle and the Open Sky, Part Eight

The finale. The Lotus Sutra and the Great Perfection face each other directly: what the series' readings share, where the two traditions honestly differ, and the strongest objections at full force, from elitism to the slogan that excuses harm. Then back to the burning house and the open sky, and to the two sentences the series began with. A comparative reading with its limits named, not a claim that two traditions are identical. View-level study only: no practice instruction, empowerment or transmission.
