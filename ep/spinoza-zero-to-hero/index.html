Episode 82

Spinoza: God or Nature

24 October 2026 · 62:02

An own-words introduction to Baruch Spinoza: the excommunicated lens grinder of Amsterdam, the Ethics built like geometry from definitions to blessedness, God or Nature, the mind as the body's idea, the emotions as physics, the Treatise that argues freedom of thought and speech is the foundation of every free state, the long afterlife from herem to Einstein, an honest side-by-side with Buddhist philosophy - where the two genuinely meet and where they do not - and a reading guide to the translations. Narrated by Tara, an AI voice. Provisional interpretation, not an authoritative translation, lineage transmission or practice authorization.

All quotations from Spinoza are read in R. H. M. Elwes's 1883 translation (public domain, Project Gutenberg #3800 and #989) and were verified verbatim against those texts; the Ethics definitions and propositions and the Theological-Political Treatise preface quotes follow Elwes's wording exactly. Biographical claims were checked against the standard public record (the Spinoza House biography and the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy), and the one episode where the record is uncertain - how close Spinoza stood to Jan de Witt - is stated as uncertain in the episode. The chapter on Buddhism is an interpretive comparison, not a claim of historical contact or influence, and its disagreements are stated as disagreements.
