# The Hand That Turns the Mirror
## Sources, rights and interpretation notes

An original fourteen-chapter course on agency, karma, recognition and the usefulness of teaching. Written 16 November 2026. Everyday scenes and examples are invented, not biographical accounts. "Agency without sovereignty" is our comparative phrase, not a traditional quotation. The course distinguishes causal influence, independent authorship, the experience of effort, and recognition. It does not claim to settle every modern free-will theory.

Not a pointing-out instruction, initiation, practice manual, attainment test or substitute for a qualified teacher. No restricted practice instructions are reproduced. No claim to have read the full 2025 revised Mirror book. No medical or mental-health advice; spiritual practice does not replace appropriate care, safety or consent.

## Source ledger

1. **Chogyal Namkhai Norbu, "The Mirror: Advice on Presence and Awareness," Religions 4(3),412-422,9 September 2013.** Translated from Tibetan by Adriano Clemente. Primary authored text, journal-published excerpt. https://www.mdpi.com/2077-1444/4/3/412 . The live page credits the author and translator; its acknowledgment records Shang Shung's authorization to publish part of the earlier 1983 work. The live footer states ©2013 by the authors, licensee MDPI, Creative Commons Attribution 3.0: http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . Used for the relation of presence, recognition, conduct and compassion, and the distinction between conceptual no-I and lived understanding. One short direct quotation on conduct. We omit its practical instructions and nested quotations from other texts.

2. **Shang Shung Publications, The Mirror Revised and Expanded Edition: Advice on Presence and Awareness.** Official publisher description, copyright year 2025,128 pages, ISBN 9788878348363. https://www.shangshungpublications.com/en/products/product/the-mirror-revised-and-expanded-edition . Used only to identify the newer edition and its reported translation corrections and expanded oral commentary. This book was not purchased or read in full. The earlier journal text is not represented as the revised edition.

3. **Tittha Sutta, "Sectarians," Anguttara Nikaya 3:62, translated by Thanissaro Bhikkhu.** https://www.dhammatalks.org/suttas/AN/AN3_62.html . Primary translation. Other numbering systems commonly label this discourse 3.61. Used for its criticism of blanket all-past-action, creator and causeless accounts and the practical problem of inaction. We do not claim this alone resolves all contemporary determinism debates.

4. **Nibbedhika Sutta, "Penetrative," AN 6:63, translated by Thanissaro Bhikkhu.** https://www.dhammatalks.org/suttas/AN/AN6_63.html . Primary translation. Used for intention as kamma, contact as its condition, bodily/verbal/mental action, and results at different times. Six-word direct quote. We preserve the distinction between the traditional rebirth framework and modern habit-only readings.

5. **Sivaka Sutta, "To Sivaka," SN 36:21, translated by Thanissaro Bhikkhu.** https://www.dhammatalks.org/suttas/SN/SN36_21.html . Primary translation with separate translator introduction. Used for the refusal to explain all feeling solely by past action and the range of conditions named. Ancient humoral categories are historical, not current medical guidance. The translator's account of old/new kamma is explicitly not presented as the text's own wording.

6. **Attakarin Sutta, "The Self-doer," AN 6:38, translated by Thanissaro Bhikkhu.** https://www.dhammatalks.org/suttas/AN/AN6_38.html . Primary translation. Used for the challenge to a declaration of no self-doing or other-doing, with initiative, perseverance and effort. We do not treat its everyday action language as proof of an independent eternal soul.

7. **Cetana Sutta, "Intention," SN 12:38, translated by Thanissaro Bhikkhu.** https://www.dhammatalks.org/suttas/SN/SN12_38.html . Primary translation. Used to complicate a blanket celebration of agency: intending, arranging and underlying fixation support the continuation of becoming. Not turned into an instruction to abandon ordinary planning.

8. **Thanissaro Bhikkhu, "The Buddha on Determinism."** https://www.dhammatalks.org/books/uncollected/Determinism.html . Contemporary interpretive essay, not a claim of scholarly consensus. Used as background for the difference between meaningful present effort and a blanket past-determination account. The course deliberately makes narrower claims than a final metaphysical verdict.

9. **Yongey Mingyur Rinpoche, "Moving Beyond Meditation," Lion's Roar,14 August 2024.** https://www.lionsroar.com/moving-beyond-meditation/ . First-person recollection of learning from Tulku Urgyen Rinpoche and other teachers. One short quotation is explicitly attributed to Mingyur's report of his father, not an independently verified recording of Tulku Urgyen. Used for nonmanufacture together with familiarization, bodhichitta, and distinctions among understanding, experience and realization. Practical pointing-out dialogue is omitted. Not evidence of a clinical anxiety cure.

10. **Namkhai Norbu teaching, The Mirror newspaper, issue 83,November/December 2006, page 2.** https://melong.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/Mirror83c.pdf . Official International Dzogchen Community publication. Used for the conceptual mirror analogy: reflections depend on conditions, while capacity is not identical to a particular reflection. One short quotation. The surrounding teaching includes practices not reproduced in this course. Exact page placement is based on the publication's own contents and text extraction, not a fresh pixel inspection of the PDF. No claim that general publication supplies transmission.

11. **Mark Siderits, "Buddha," Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, substantive revision 6 March 2023.** https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/buddha/ . Scholarly interpretation used for background on nonself, conventional persons, causal continuity and problems of agency. Not treated as agreement by every Buddhist tradition. We do not adopt a casual cosmic-justice account as a judgment on anyone's suffering.

12. **Swami Lakshmanjoo, "Acting and Will in Kashmir Shaivism," official Academy transcript excerpt,14 April 2025.** https://www.lakshmanjooacademy.org/blog/acting-and-will-in-kashmir-shaivism-explained-by-swami-lakshmanjoo . The site identifies this as an audio transcript, not the book text, connected to chapter 10 of Kashmir Shaivism: The Secret Supreme. Used for the distinction between divine independence and dependent individual action, with one four-word quotation. The difficult responsibility/right-and-wrong discussion is acknowledged and bounded, not suppressed or converted into immunity from accountability. Copyright John Hughes.

13. **John Hughes, "The difference between energy and its holder in Kashmir Shaivism," Lakshmanjoo Academy,25 November 2024.** https://www.lakshmanjooacademy.org/blog/the-difference-between-energy-and-its-holder-in-kashmir-shaivism . Official introduction to a published commentary, not a direct quotation from a classical scripture. Used for a contrasting Shaiva mirror cosmology and divine freedom. This is not equated with Buddhist emptiness or rigpa. Copyright John Hughes.

## Rendered editions

Synthetic Aoede narration, presented as Tara. Narration duration 38:00. All 28 clip transcripts were compared with the frozen manuscript; suspicious passages had independent recognizer checks. Three full replacement clips were selected. All 27 assembled joins were checked by transcript. This is automated and targeted review, not full human-ear listening or native-name certification. Specialist names use English-friendly approximations.

The public MP3 is 48 kHz mono, 128 kbps, measured -17.97 LUFS and -3.11 dBTP. Only fixed gain (+2.8 dB) and transparent oversampled peak limiting were applied. No EQ, pitch or speed change. The untouched 24 kHz source master and all 36 original and alternate takes are preserved privately. Earlier 24 kHz-output MP3 experiments failed true-peak checks and are not used.

The original moving edition contains fourteen symbolic animated scenes, no borrowed images or sacred diagrams. Actual encoded frames from every chapter were inspected for layout and the intended scene. Motion was measured in all fourteen chapter interiors. The complete delivery video decoded without errors. Its AAC soundtrack measures -17.65 LUFS and -1.42 dBTP. No animation is offered as proof of the philosophical claims or as a practice instruction.
