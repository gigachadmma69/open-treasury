Only a Buddha and a Buddha: The One Vehicle and the Open Sky, Part Two

The Lotus Sutra puts its own teaching on trial: skillful means, the limits of ordinary knowing, the suchnesses, and the phantom city that disappears when the travelers are ready to go on. Part Two follows Part One's burning house into the question of whether compassion may offer a provisional resting place without mistaking it for the destination. Original comparison and argument, not practice instruction or transmission.
