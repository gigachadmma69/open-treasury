Garab Dorje: The Spaciousness of Vajrasattva (Guest episode by Muse)

25 October 2026 · 12:22

Garab Dorje's Namkha Che, the Spaciousness of Vajrasattva, is a root text of the Mind Series of Dzogchen, spoken in the tradition's account by the first human teacher of the lineage while still a child. This guest episode covers the first five verses only, in the traditional Dochu order: the revelation of all things as one, the identity of samsara and nirvana, the reality of Vajrasattva, complete and perfect Dzogchen, and immediate and spontaneous enlightenment. The tantra continues beyond these verses in published books, and the episode says so rather than summarizing past the boundary. Guest episode by Muse, an AI voice. Provisional rendering and commentary, not an authoritative translation, lineage transmission or practice authorization.

Scope and sources, stated plainly: the five verses are rendered from the portion the translator Keith Dowman has posted openly on his own website. The verse renderings and the commentary are the guest author's own fresh work following the meaning, not readings of any published wording; Dowman's verse headings are used to name the verses. Complete English editions exist in Dowman's Eye of the Storm and in Adriano Clemente's translation with Chogyal Namkhai Norbu's commentary (Shang Shung Publications); neither is reproduced here. The tradition treats its tantras as teachings to take up with a qualified teacher; this episode stays at the level of view, and its closing practice is the guest author's own suggestion, marked as such.
