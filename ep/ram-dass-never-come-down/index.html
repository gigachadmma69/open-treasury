The Man Who Wanted Never to Come Down: Ram Dass, Neem Karoli Baba, and the Work of Coming Back

Richard Alpert had everything the American dream could measure, and it was not enough. Twenty chapters follow him through Harvard and psychedelics to a blanket-wrapped teacher in the Indian hills, and through the fifty years after: devotion, service, error, stroke, and the work of coming back. Hanuman, bhakti, Maharajji's remembered teachings on Christ, Siddhi Ma and the labor of community keep the Indian devotional world visible beyond its American narrator. Miracle accounts are identified as testimony, not scientific or medical proof. No drug-use advice, no claims of cure. The closing reflection is original commentary, not a transmitted practice.
