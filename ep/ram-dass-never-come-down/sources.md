# Source and accuracy companion
## The Man Who Wanted Never to Come Down
Research checked 22 November 2026. This companion supports an original, interpretive audio course. It is not a critical edition or a complete reading of the books named below.

## Evidence rules

Public events are distinguished from a participant's recollection and from devotional belief. Miracle accounts are attributed, never presented as scientifically established. Interpretation and optional reflection are the narrator's own. No modern source is read at length, no third-party audio is included, and no claim is made to have read a complete book when only a publisher description, archive page, or excerpt was inspected.

## Accuracy decisions and remaining limits

- This is a substantial introductory and intermediate course, not an exhaustive archival biography of Neem Karoli Baba or a book-by-book commentary on Ram Dass's entire corpus.
- Maharajji's early life is not filled with attractive but weakly verified dates or childhood episodes. The course says explicitly that the English-language historical record consulted is uneven.
- No independent verification was found for paranormal knowledge, the train miracle, drug immunity, voluntary control of death, or postmortem intervention. None is asserted as fact.
- Ram Dass's interpretations of grace, soul, guru and love are not presented as established medical or psychological findings.
- No drug use advice, dosing, meditation cure, or replacement for professional care.
- Service credits remain collective. No claim that a single teacher personally produced an organization's medical outcomes.
- Sources do not establish every private detail of the Harvard incident. The documented research-boundary breach is stated; a sexual relationship is not alleged.
- Audio pronunciation, completeness, audio levels, playable duration, publication destinations and feed ingestion remain separate final checks. Script clearance is not release clearance.

## Rights decisions

All narration is original English commentary, paraphrase, synthesis and expressly marked original reflection. No modern translation is recited, no protected musical performance or recording is included, and no source image is bundled. Short book titles, personal names and common religious terms identify their subjects. The course does not reproduce a substantial passage from any source.

The original course text can carry the Treasury's normal CC0 dedication for its own newly authored material. That dedication does not extend to linked sources, trademarks, book covers, photographs, recordings, or the works discussed. The source websites' accessibility does not establish permission to copy them. The package contains no source-page dumps, pirated book files, or acquired third-party media. Do not imply endorsement by the estates, foundations, ashrams or interview publishers.
