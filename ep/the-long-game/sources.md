## Chapter 1-2: the data

1. Bellis MA, Hennell T, Lushey C, Hughes K, Tocque K, Ashton JR. "Elvis to Eminem: quantifying the price of fame through early mortality of European and North American rock and pop stars." J Epidemiol Community Health 2007;61(10):896. https://jech.bmj.com/content/61/10/896 . Read on the page 15 Nov 2026: n=1064 stars from All-Time Top 1000 albums; from 3 to 25 years post fame, mortality more than 1.7x demographically matched US/UK populations; European stars' relative mortality returns to population levels after 25 years. Data, not quoted prose.
2. Hill PL, Turiano NA (2014), reported by the Association for Psychological Science: https://www.psychologicalscience.org/news/releases/having-a-sense-of-purpose-in-life-may-add-years-to-your-life.html . MIDUS, 6,000+ participants, 14-year follow-up, 569 deaths (~9%); purpose predicted lower mortality at every age, held after controlling for positive relations and emotions, and regardless of retirement status. Hill quote in script is a lightly compressed paraphrase of his press statement; treat as paraphrase.
3. Cohen R, Bavishi C, Rozanski A. "Purpose in Life and Its Relationship to All-Cause Mortality and Cardiovascular Events: A Meta-Analysis." Psychosom Med 2016;78(2):122-33. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26630073/ . Abstract read 15 Nov 2026: ten prospective studies, 136,265 participants; adjusted pooled RR 0.83 (CI 0.75-0.91) for all-cause mortality and 0.83 (0.75-0.92) for cardiovascular events.

## Chapter 11: the deathless

39. Mahaparinibbana Sutta (DN 16), Pali text: Anandajoti Bhikkhu's edition, https://ancient-buddhist-texts.net/Buddhist-Texts/D16-Mahaparinibbana/D16-Mahaparinibbana.pdf . Verified in Pali 15 Nov 2026: "so akankhamano ananda tathagato kappam va tittheyya kappavasesam va" (the kappa/kappavasesa passage, with translation disputed as eon vs proper/full lifespan); Ananda, "marena pariyutthitacitto" (mind possessed by Mara), fails to request; the Buddha renounces the life-principle at Capala and announces three months; final words "handa dani bhikkhave amantayami vo: vayadhamma sankhara, appamadena sampadetha" with the note "ayam tathagatassa pacchima vaca" (this was the Tathagata's final utterance). English renderings in the script are the author's own from the Pali. Pali canon is public domain.
