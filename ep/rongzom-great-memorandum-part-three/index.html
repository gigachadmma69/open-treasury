Reading Rongzom | Part Three

What Counts as a Thing: Reading Rongzom, Part Three

CORRECTION, November 27, 2026: This episode says the memorandum records twenty-one substances with mind among them. On rechecking the printed page, that number is not settled word for word in either Tibetan witness we can read, so please treat the count of twenty-one as unconfirmed. The rest of the episode is unchanged. The audio itself was not edited.

A beginner introduction to Rongzom Chokyi Zangpo's Great Memorandum on the View, centered on the passage that defines substance (rdzas, Sanskrit dravya): the worldly etymology of goods that can be grasped and cut, the Dharma-side etymology in which the teaching transforms the mind, the boundary cases that fail to count as substance, and a count of substances that this episode could not confirm (see the correction). Variants between the two Tibetan witnesses are explained for newcomers as they occur.
