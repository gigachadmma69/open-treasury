# Reading Rongzom, Part Three: source credits

Great Memorandum on the View (lta ba'i brjed byang chen mo) by Rongzom Chokyi Zangpo. The short passages read in this episode are The Open Treasury's own study renderings from the Tibetan: AI work offered for scholar review, not a definitive translation, and not a paraphrase of any existing English translation.

Working witness: Leh gsung thor bu print, Buddhist Digital Resource Center MW30125, image volume I5886, printed pages 203-204 (scans 215-216).
https://library.bdrc.io/show/bdr:MW30125_DC9241

Comparison witness: modern typeset edition, BDRC W29621, consulted through the OpenPecha e-text repository I47678AE1.
https://github.com/Openpecha-Data/I47678AE1

Locus and variant record (abridged). Transition into the substance section, printed p. 204 recto, line 1 (residual: bar gi vs ba'i). Worldly etymology "gzung zhing bcad du rung ba'i phyir nor rdzas zhes bya ste" with purpose clause "don che ba'i phyir" (Leh reads bcad, W29621 reads btsad; Leh don che, W29621 don tshe). Gold and silver example confirmed only to the page edge. Not-substance list opening "drang pa'i rdo bo rtso ba la sogs pa" (W29621: drad pa'i rdo btson la sogs pa), left open. Dharma-side etymology "de bzhin du bstan chos kyis blo bsgyur ba" and "blo bsgyur ba'i gang zag". Counts: "sems pa yang rdzas su yod do", "gcig kyang yod de", "rdzas nyi shu rtsa gcig zhes 'dod do" (residual on nyi/ni). The full twenty-one-item enumeration is not yet verified and is not read in this episode.
