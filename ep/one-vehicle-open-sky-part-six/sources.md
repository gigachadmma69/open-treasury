Honesty notes:
1. Paraphrases of Longchenpa's commentary (Phyogs bcu mun sel) are from Dorje's rendering of the commentary section for text 2.16 and 2.1; one short quotation ("One's own mind-as-such is originally pure like the sky") is verbatim from that rendering.
2. "Five degenerations" list in the script is standard background (era, view, afflictions, beings, lifespan); it is not from the verified quotes. Dorje's text names "the five degenerations" only.
3. Part 5's hand-forward called the Guhyagarbha the "root tantra" of the Great Perfection's tradition; this part corrects it to "principal tantra of Nyingma Mahayoga, read beside and in light of the Great Perfection" (Dorje's own contents list separates Mahayoga and Atiyoga interpretations).
4. Eighth-century transmission to Tibet is presented as tradition, not as settled history.
5. Lotus lines: "fancy that it is burning", "teeming with gods and men" are Kern's wording. Triple-world line is from the Lifespan chapter (Kern); chapter number not stated in the script because numbering differs across editions.
6. The "Lhasa" example paraphrases Dorje's rendering of the commentary to text 2.1, kept at view level.
7. Hand-forward (Part 7 Seventeen Voices, One Throat; Part 8 confrontation) matches the master brief.
8. No claim that Vima Nyingtik material contains two-truths terminology (grep of staged text found none).
