The Guhyagarbha, or The Two Truths at the Junction: The One Vehicle and the Open Sky, Part Six
Released 22 November 2026.

Narrated by Tara using the Aoede AI voice. All 21 takes were machine-transcribed with two independent models and compared with their actual take texts. Base-model word-sequence agreement ranged from 63.1% to 100% per take; the lowest scores were recognizer dropouts that disappeared under a second, larger model or a wider beam, and the largest apparent gaps moved to different places in different runs, which marks them as recognizer limits rather than missing narration. Best-run agreement on every take was at least 91.0%. No missing substantive passage was identified. The one recurring difference is the recognizer's spelling of the translator's name. Final listening copy: 29:23, -17.0 LUFS integrated, -0.7 dBTP true peak, 128 kbps mono; complete decode verified. No human listened end to end, and no native-speaker pronunciation review was done. Transcription checks cannot certify every pronunciation or small audible artifact.

21 takes joined with 0.9-second pauses. Peaks limited (0.58), then loudness-normalized (linear, two-pass) to about -17 LUFS.
