The Guhyagarbha, or The Two Truths at the Junction: The One Vehicle and the Open Sky, Part Six

The series crosses to the other mountain. A few verses from the second chapter of the Guhyagarbha Tantra, read in Gyurme Dorje's English, are set beside the Lotus Sutra's own sentence about a world creatures imagine to be burning while the Buddhafield teems with gods and men. Void and pure fields in one breath, creation without creation, and knots tied in the sky: does the junction between the road and the ground hold from the ground side? Includes an honest correction to Part Five's description of the tantra. View-level study only: the Guhyagarbha is a sealed tantra, and nothing here is practice instruction, empowerment or a substitute for a qualified teacher.
