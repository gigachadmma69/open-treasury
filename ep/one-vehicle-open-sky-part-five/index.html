The Lifespan and the Dragon Girl: The One Vehicle and the Open Sky, Part Five

The Lotus Sutra breaks its own clock, twice. An eight-year-old naga's daughter attains Buddhahood in a single moment while the old order watches in silence, and the Buddha reveals a lifespan that outlasts the math of dust atoms: his awakening was countless eons ago, and his death itself is skillful means. The part ends where the sutra says the triple world is not born and does not die, the Great Perfection's ground in the Lotus's own words. Original comparison and argument, not practice instruction or transmission.
