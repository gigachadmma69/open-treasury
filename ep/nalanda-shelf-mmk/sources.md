# Sources: The Nalanda Shelf, Book One - Nagarjuna's Mulamadhyamakakarika

- Mulamadhyamakakarika, Sanskrit e-text: GRETIL (Goettingen Register of Electronic Texts in Indian Languages), used as witness for the root verses.
- Chandrakirti, Prasannapada (Lucid Words): informs the commentary and is credited aloud in every installment.
- Checked against, without borrowing wording: Mark Siderits and Shoryu Katsura, Nagarjuna's Middle Way (Wisdom Publications, 2013); Jay L. Garfield, The Fundamental Wisdom of the Middle Way (Oxford University Press, 1995); David J. Kalupahana, Mulamadhyamakakarika of Nagarjuna (Motilal Banarsidass, 1991).

The translation of the root verses and all commentary are The Open Treasury's own renderings from the Sanskrit. Released CC0 1.0 Universal.
