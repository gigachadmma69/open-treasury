Nagarjuna: Zero to Hero

22 October 2026 · 1:08:37

An own-words introduction to Nagarjuna and Madhyamaka: svabhava, dependent arising, emptiness, analysis of motion and time, the two truths, self, Buddha, nirvana and later debates. Narrated by Tara, an AI voice. Original explanatory narration, not a reading of a commercial translation. Provisional interpretation, not an authoritative translation or lineage transmission. MMK verses are paraphrased; verse numbers follow the scriptwriter's memory of Sanskrit numbering and were not independently checked for this release. Historical legends, proposed Greek contacts, and disputed readings are labeled as such. Comparisons with Hume, Zeno, Wittgenstein and physics are parallels, not influence or scientific equivalence. Rangtong/shentong, ineffability and logical readings remain live debates. The Dzogchen connection is presented as a tradition's view, not as Nagarjuna's own teaching.
