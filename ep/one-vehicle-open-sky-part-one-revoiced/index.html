The Burning House and the Open Sky: The One Vehicle and the Open Sky, Part One (Re-voiced)

The Lotus Sutra says everyone without exception awakens. Garab Dorje says there is nothing to do and nowhere to go. Part One of The One Vehicle and the Open Sky asks whether those are the same claim: Vulture Peak, the walkout of the five thousand, the one vehicle declared, the burning house parable, the Three Statements That Strike the Vital Point, and the objections both texts invite. View-level study only: nothing here is a practice instruction, an empowerment, or a substitute for a qualified teacher. Some quoted texts are marked restricted by their own traditions; they are discussed, not transmitted.
