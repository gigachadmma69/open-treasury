# Ishaputra: the man behind the snow
## Sources and listening guide

An original English listening course by Tara. Approx. 7,600 words, intended for about one hour. This is an introduction to public material, not a lineage transmission or practice authorization. The original explanatory prose is offered as CC0; underlying source texts, subtitles and videos retain their own rights. No source video, soundtrack, or transcript is reproduced in the course.

## Start with these three public talks
These are editorial picks for clarity, range and fit, not a ranking of the entire channel or a verified popularity chart. English transcript panels were read in full on the live YouTube pages on 22 October 2026. The subtitle translation has not been independently checked against the complete Hindi audio. The course does not claim that the source videos were watched in full.

1. Meditation for Capacity Building
   Kaulantak Peeth, 29 November 2017. 21:52.
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sk1OX2rYUJo
   Read: full English transcript. Themes: freshness rather than mechanical repetition; changes in environment; imagery and sound as practice tools; observing and explaining an experience in one's own words.
   Useful sections: 3:35-10:38, stale versus fresh mind; 13:52-16:07, breath and imagery (described, not prescribed in this course); 17:12-20:59, observation and articulation.

2. Sick religion & spirituality
   Kaulantak Peeth, 21 November 2017. 23:14.
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vRmNdGV0TiA
   Read: full English transcript. Themes: manoloka (mental world); imagination as an instrument; experiences do not automatically prove realization; warnings about instant attainment and sectarian certainty.
   Useful sections: 1:32-3:09, outer and inner life; 7:01-9:47, imagination and culturally shaped accounts; 11:40-18:51, spiritual experiences and imagination as a tool; 19:55-20:47, respectful listening to other teachers.
   Caveat: some sweeping statements about religions, scientific knowledge and mental illness are polemical, not verified research or clinical judgments. The course preserves the argument without adopting those statements as facts.

3. Doctor Treatment and Patient - चिकित्सक चिकित्सा और रोगी
   Kaulantak Peeth, 6 December 2017. 25:54.
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nj6fQxgSB4s
   Read: full English transcript. Themes: illness, grief, human relationships, prayer as support, medical care, illness without shame, rejection of caste-based exclusion.
   Useful sections: 5:10-8:44, grief and relationships; 11:45-16:43, prayer, support and peace even when cure is impossible; 17:19-19:38, illness without shame and consulting doctors; 20:44-23:50, caste and spiritual access; 24:00-25:45, humane medical care.
   Caveat: claims about positivity, energy, prayer, karma and physical outcomes are religious views, not established medical effects. No replacement of medical treatment is recommended. Grief is not a failure.

## Identity and institutional context
4. Kaulantak Nath, Siddhapedia, 9 October 2018.
   https://siddhapedia.com/kaulantak-nath
   Read in full. Identifies the current Kaulantak Nath as Mahayogi Satyendra Nath (Ishaputra); explains the office, guru tradition, institutional authority, and the distinction between instruction and guaranteed realization.
   Source type: tradition-affiliated public encyclopedia. High confidence for institutional self-description; its religious and historical origin claims are not independently verified.

5. Mahasiddha Ishaputra, Kaulantak Peeth, dated 1 June 2020 in fetched page.
   https://kaulantakpeeth.com/service/mahasiddha-ishaputra/
   Read. Institutional biography gives birth name Satish Thakur, spiritual name Satyendra Nath, and training narrative. Devotional biography, not independent attainment verification.

6. Mahasiddha Ishaputra: Descent of Divinity in the Human Form, IKSVP, 10 September 2023.
   https://iksvp.com/mahasiddha-ishaputra-descent-of-divinity-in-the-human-form/
   Read in full. Establishes relationship of Ishaputra, Kaulantak Peeth and the educational institution; describes the thirty-eight-guru training account. Divine-incarnation claims are attributed beliefs.

7. International Kaulantak Siddha Vidyapeeth, home and public encyclopedia gateway.
   https://iksvp.com/
   https://iksvp.com/siddha-pedia/
   Read. Establishes educational mission and links the institution to the Siddhapedia entries below. Institutional text, not independent historical scholarship.

## Deep cuts: public philosophical overviews
These describe the tradition's system. They are not all direct words of Ishaputra. The course identifies source genre and distinguishes its own analogies and interpretation. Restricted/initiation-dependent ritual details, secret sequences, invocations and mantra procedures are not taught.

8. Jeeva Pash Mukti, Siddhapedia, 18 October 2023.
   https://siddhapedia.com/jeeva-pash-mukti/
   Read in full. Bondage and liberation; six categories include attachment to experiences and divine states. Explicit Atman/Brahman framework is retained rather than recast as Buddhist selflessness. Initiation and ritual procedures omitted.

9. Tri Siddhatva Patala, Siddhapedia, 14 November 2023.
   https://siddhapedia.com/tri-siddhatva-patala
   Read in full. Knowledge-oriented, worldly-responsibility and renunciant approaches; traditional story of debate followed by integration. The course treats the story as an account within the tradition, not independently established ancient history.

10. Tejottama Yoga Patala, Siddhapedia, 20 December 2023.
    https://siddhapedia.com/tejottama-yoga-patala/
    Read in full. Kala Deha, Teja Deha and Brahm Deha; integration of form, luminosity and Brahman. Explicitly secretive practice sequence and practical instructions omitted. No equation with a Buddhist three-body system.

11. Deva Mahamudra, Siddhapedia, 27 July 2023.
    https://siddhapedia.com/deva-mahamudra/
    Read in full. Institution-specific Deva Parampara culmination, described as one of twenty-one mahamudras. Claims of historical priority over other traditions are not endorsed. Not equated with Buddhist Mahamudra. Ritual/eye movement practices not taught.

12. Timirantakari Dakini, Siddhapedia, 2 December 2023.
    https://siddhapedia.com/timirantakari-dakini/
    Read in full. Public sacred narrative of the night goddess, foxes, local protection, women transmitters, and the article's critique of specialists monopolizing power. Healing claims not treated as medical facts; invocations and worship procedures omitted.

3. Do not mistake the movie for freedom - manoloka, imagination, visions, discrimination and teacher pluralism.

6. The hospital test - relationships, care, medical humility, caste exclusion and final listening path.

## Remaining limits
No exhaustive catalogue, measured popularity ranking, full Hindi-audio cross-check, independent confirmation of supernatural attainments, or academic verification of the institution's ancient lineage claims is claimed. Strong direct talks are distinguished from institution-authored explanations. All analogies and reflective questions invented for the course are identified as Tara's own interpretation. This course does not establish whether Ishaputra is "powerful" in a supernatural sense.
