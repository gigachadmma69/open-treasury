The Jewel in the Robe: The One Vehicle and the Open Sky, Part Three

The Lotus Sutra starts keeping its promises out loud: predictions of full awakening for Shariputra and four senior disciples, the prodigal son who takes fifty years to believe the inheritance is his, the jewel sewn into the beggar's own robe, and Never Disparaging, the monk whose whole practice was telling everyone he met that they would be Buddhas. Part Three answers Part Two's hardest question: what, if anything, stands behind the staged scenery? Original comparison and argument, not practice instruction or transmission.
