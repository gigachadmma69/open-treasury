The Fly Out of the Bottle: Ludwig Wittgenstein

26 October 2026 · 58:44

The Fly Out of the Bottle: Ludwig Wittgenstein - the full eight-chapter course. The Vienna house and what it cost, the aeronaut who walked into Russell's rooms, the Tractatus and its claim that the problems of philosophy are in essentials finally solved, the schoolmaster of Trattenbach, the turn away from the picture theory, meaning as use, philosophy as therapy, the hinges of On Certainty, and the fly's way out of the bottle. This is the complete course, distinct from the earlier short crash course on the feed (Wittgenstein: Meaning Is Use). An independent critical listening course: Tractatus quotations use C. K. Ogden's 1922 English translation, quotations are short and attributed, Wittgenstein's later works are presumed in copyright and are described rather than excerpted, and this release grants no rights in any underlying work. Narrated by Tara, an AI voice. Original narration and commentary CC0 1.0 Universal.
