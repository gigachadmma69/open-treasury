# Garab Dorje: The First Hand (Corrected Source-Stacked Release)
Released6November2026. A new57-minute source-stacked course, not a silent replacement of the earlier First Hand recording. The earlier episode and GUID remain unchanged. Narrated by Tara, an AI voice, with narration produced by Muse.

Traditional biography is attributed to named textual witnesses, not presented as independently verified modern history. This is a bounded study of six source pages, not an exhaustive account of every surviving Garab text, a new Tibetan translation or a manuscript collation. The Sanskrit backformation/name-origin chronology and false Khyentse identity are not repeated. G2 is site-marked Restricted. We discuss literary attribution, lineage and nonprocedural doctrine only; no empowerment, pointing-out instruction, posture/gaze/breath sequence or practice authorization is provided.
