## Attribution and uncertainty
Miracles, recognition stories and moon visions are traditional accounts, not independently established events. Naming accounts differ and are kept distinct. Later rangtong/zhentong labels are not treated as settled explanations of every writing. Received attribution and autograph are different kinds of evidence. Reviews are identified as reviews; no full-read claim is made for the commercial books they review. Comparative ethical examples are our discussion, not invented recorded speech by the Third Karmapa.
