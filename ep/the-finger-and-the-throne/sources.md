# The Finger and the Throne

Adi Da, Non-Separation, and the Question of Authority

The Open Treasury. Source companion for an original 22-chapter course. Research checked 18 November 2026. Narration is original commentary, not a reading or translation of Adi Da's books. This document records what was actually consulted, what kinds of claims those sources can support, and the limits still open.

## The course's central distinction

An illuminating passage, a transformative experience, a metaphysical account, and a claim to exclusive personal authority are different things. The course takes the teaching seriously without treating either devotional recognition or critical accusation as a universal verdict. Its governing question is whether a reader can receive an insight without surrendering judgment about its author.

The teaching chapters are grounded chiefly in Adidam's published Understanding talk, its two-page seven-stage exposition, official biography, lineage account and FAQ. These are authoritative evidence of the movement's own presentation, not independent confirmation of divinity, supernatural efficacy, clinical claims, or safety.

The critical chapters draw on Scott Lowe's retrospective participant memoir and religious-studies reflection; David Lane's essay and later reassessment; contemporary and later reporting; and the movement's own response. Each is kept within its evidential scope. No complete court docket or authenticated disposal order was obtained. No present membership census or governance audit was completed.

No attempt has been made to explain why particular booklets appear at a particular cafe. Their location does not establish the owner's motives, affiliations, or endorsement.

## Claims and boundaries

Self-contraction is explained as a teaching, not diagnosed in the listener. Defensive loops can be examined without blaming illness, poverty, coercion, bereavement or injury on the person who suffers. Deliberate skill, treatment, practical care and repair are not dismissed as inferior seeking.

Heart and Conscious Light are theological/metaphysical terms in this context. No neuroscience, physics or clinical validation is claimed. No inward experience is treated as sufficient proof of an exclusive teacher's complete metaphysics or safety.

The seven-stage model is the movement's map. Its approximate early developmental rhythm is not a clinical timetable. Other religious traditions are not reduced to their assigned place within that map. No attainment is diagnosed or promised.

The official Understanding pages are a later published presentation of a talk dated 25 April 1972. Their editorial framing and terminology should not be mistaken for an independently verified contemporaneous recording. All four pages were read, including the difficult questioner exchange and its editorial framing.

The lineage discussion does not deny that a teaching permission was claimed or reproduced. It separates that scope from appointment as successor or validation of later exclusive avatar claims. No original-language, handwriting, forensic or documentary authentication was performed.

Allegations of harm remain attributed allegations. A complaint is a pleading, not a finding. Settlement, dismissal and a later witness statement do not automatically decide every factual dispute. The movement's defense is presented as its defense, not independent exoneration. The course avoids exact procedural conclusions because the needed orders were not obtained.

Organizational policy claims are explicitly not an audit of implementation. Historical accounts do not establish every current participant's experience. No current numerical membership estimate is given.

The listener is not invited to undertake a crisis, ordeal, surrender of boundaries, fasting, sexual practice, or abandonment of care. The concluding reflection is an ordinary communication exercise authored for this course, not initiation into Adidam or a reconstruction of its formal practice.

## Rights, narration and release status

Short technical terms and book titles identify the subject matter. Sources retain their own rights; public access is not permission to republish their text. A phrase-overlap screen is a diagnostic, not a legal clearance or guarantee.
