# The Crystal and the Open Sky
## Studying Longchenpa on Awareness

1 November 2026. A 61-minute study companion, with an introduction, seven units and conclusion. Independent source-witness, divergence and final audio checks completed before release.

This original study companion follows selected explanatory prose and eleven short scriptural citations from chapter ten of Longchenpa's autocommentary, Lung gi gter mdzod, on the Treasury of the Basic Space of Phenomena. It is not a complete reading of the chapter or of the cited scriptures. English readings are provisional study renderings, not a certified translation. The English recording that prompted this study was already known before the renderings were made.

## What is included
- An introduction to the vocabulary and textual layers.
- Seven connected units of explanation, with the cited readings interleaved.
- Eleven citations attributed locally to the All-Creating King (four), the samadhi chapter (one), Great Garuda (one), Lion's Perfect Power (three), Pearl Garland (one), and Ratnakuta/Heap of Jewels (one).
- A conclusion connecting the crystal, dream, grasped object and grasping mind, the distinction between ordinary mind and wisdom, and the retention of absorption.

The Great Garuda's third line remains unresolved and is not supplied as a complete English translation. Full bibliographic identity of the samadhi chapter and the exact sutra within the Ratnakuta collection remain unverified. Source names are kept at the level actually established.

A, B, F, G, I and J match the typed packet; no missing or added prose blocks were found in the selected range. Comparison was a close reading, not a character-level critical edition. For F-K the typed comparison witness was the review packet generated from the chapter, not a second fresh wiki extraction.

Edition differences and decisions:
- C: printed lta ba la versus online lar. We follow the printed la.
- D: printed rgyal po yin versus online yi. We follow yin, giving the opening a separate predication: the teacher's essence is the all-creating king. This revises the earlier genitive construction. The printed page has apparatus circles whose notes lie outside the checked page range, plus a possible ti-like sign after ngo bo that the reader could not identify securely. We do not incorporate that uncertain mark as an additional word or claim that the apparatus has been resolved. The opening English remains provisional.
- E: printed ma yengs versus online me yengs. We follow ma. The printed edition continues the preceding same-tantra quotation with ces pa dang, rather than repeating the explicit title attribution found in the online witness. The local source remains the All-Creating King.
- H: printed sangs rgyas sa versus packet sangs rgyas pa. We retain the shared intelligible sense, buddhahood, while recording the sa/pa difference without claiming a settled emendation or forcing an English distinction from it.
- K: printed mi 'dor ba ste versus packet mi 'dor ste. The extra ba does not change the English reading. Minor deng/ding sang and rol par/rol pa differences in adjacent prose likewise do not alter this paraphrase.

The independent source review verifies the Tibetan witnesses and local attributions. It is not a certification of this English translation by a qualified Tibetan translator.

## Transmission caution
Tsadra's work description says this autocommentary has traditionally been transmitted in the context of empowerment and oral instruction from a qualified teacher. This episode is textual study and commentary, not empowerment, pointing-out instruction or a substitute for a teacher. It imports no advanced posture, gaze, energy or other practice procedure from omitted portions of the chapter. Listeners should respect the tradition's transmission context.

## English comparison and production

The eleven English renderings were compared manually with the already-known public recording and its accessible transcript, not with every word of a licensed printed Barron volume. No blind Tibetan-first claim is made. This comparison and the independent Tibetan witness check were accepted for this provisional study release; they are not a translator's certification.

Narrated by Tara, an AI voice; narration produced with Muse. Original narration was supplied as v4 and passed full ASR/text comparison, suspect-window arbitration, pitch and decode checks. One producer-reported Garuda-region synthesis loop was repaired before delivery, then independently checked in the delivered recording. Final nine chapter markers were aligned to spoken word times and remuxed without changing encoded audio packets.
