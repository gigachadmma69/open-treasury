The Crystal and the Open Sky: Studying Longchenpa on Awareness

1 November 2026 | 61:28 | Introduction, seven units and conclusion

An hour-long study companion to the Gone to Suchness selection: eleven short scriptural citations and selected explanatory prose from chapter ten of Longchenpa's autocommentary. Provisional English readings are interleaved with original explanation, an opening crash course and a conclusion.

Not a complete chapter or complete tantra reading, not a certified translation and not a reading of a modern English translation. The Great Garuda's third quoted line remains incomplete in English. Some edition differences and two incomplete bibliographic identifications remain explicit. No advanced practice procedures are imported.

Transmission context

Transmission context: Tsadra describes this work as traditionally transmitted with empowerment and oral instruction from a qualified teacher. This is textual study, not empowerment, pointing-out instruction or a substitute for a teacher.
