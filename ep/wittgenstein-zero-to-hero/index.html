Wittgenstein, Zero to Hero

22 October 2026 · 1:01:29

An own-words introduction to Ludwig Wittgenstein: the Tractatus, pictures and facts, the ladder, meaning as use, language games, family resemblances, rule-following and private language. Biographical anecdotes are flagged as traditional accounts. Madhyamaka and Zen parallels are interpretive, not claims of historical influence. Famous lines are briefly paraphrased; no modern translation is read verbatim. Narrated by Tara, an AI voice. Provisional interpretation, not an authoritative translation.
