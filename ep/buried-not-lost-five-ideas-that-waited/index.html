Buried, Not Lost

Buried, Not Lost: Five Ideas That Waited

A course that checks five rediscovery stories before believing them: Evagrius and his book of talking back, the Gandharan birch bark scrolls, Paramartha's ninth consciousness, the two Dzogchen texts from the Dunhuang cave, and the Treatise on the Two Entrances credited to Bodhidharma. Each gets a hype check and a Tuesday test. One complete episode of about 62 minutes.
