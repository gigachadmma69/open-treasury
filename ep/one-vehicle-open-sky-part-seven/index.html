Seventeen Voices, One Throat: The One Vehicle and the Open Sky, Part Seven

Revised recording. The Seventeen Tantras are the heart scriptures of the Great Perfection. We have not found and checked a complete English translation this course may reproduce, so this episode says that plainly and listens to what can be verified: a short tantra from a related cycle, Longchenpa's view poem, and Mipham twice, arguing and instructing, all in Adam Pearcey's Lotsawa House translations, beside the tradition's own list of the Seventeen and the Lotus's burning house. The comparison is the course's interpretation, offered with its limits named. View-level study only: nothing here is practice instruction, empowerment or a substitute for a qualified teacher.
