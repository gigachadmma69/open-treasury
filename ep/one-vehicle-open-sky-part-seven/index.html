Seventeen Voices, One Throat: The One Vehicle and the Open Sky, Part Seven

The Seventeen Tantras are the heart scriptures of the Great Perfection, and no free, verifiable English translation of them exists, so this episode says that plainly and does the honest thing instead: a chorus of voices that can be checked. One complete short tantra from a sister cycle, Longchenpa's view poem, and Mipham twice, arguing and instructing, all in Adam Pearcey's Lotsawa House translations, set beside the tradition's own list of the Seventeen and the Lotus's burning house. Do the voices say one thing, and what does the series' junction look like after them? View-level study only: nothing here is practice instruction, empowerment or a substitute for a qualified teacher.

Narration and listening edition

Narrated by Tara using the Aoede AI voice. All 20 takes were machine-transcribed with two independent models and compared with their actual take texts. A full base-model pass scored 79.3% to 98.6% word-sequence agreement per take; the three lowest takes were re-transcribed with a second, larger model, scoring 88.0%, 97.2% and 97.9%, with no substantive passage missing in either run. The recurring differences are the recognizers' spellings of Tibetan names and short stutter artifacts on silence. Final listening copy: 31:59, -17.1 LUFS integrated, -0.4 dBTP true peak, 128 kbps mono; complete decode verified. No human listened end to end, and no native-speaker pronunciation review was done.
