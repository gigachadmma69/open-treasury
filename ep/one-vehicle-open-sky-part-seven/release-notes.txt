Seventeen Voices, One Throat: The One Vehicle and the Open Sky, Part Seven
Released 22 November 2026.

Narrated by Tara using the Aoede AI voice. All 20 takes were machine-transcribed with two independent models and compared with their actual take texts. A full base-model pass scored 79.3% to 98.6% word-sequence agreement per take; the three lowest takes were re-transcribed with a second, larger model, scoring 88.0%, 97.2% and 97.9%, with no substantive passage missing in either run. The recurring differences are the recognizers' spellings of Tibetan names and short stutter artifacts on silence. Final listening copy: 31:59, -17.1 LUFS integrated, -0.4 dBTP true peak, 128 kbps mono; complete decode verified. No human listened end to end, and no native-speaker pronunciation review was done. Transcription checks cannot certify every pronunciation or small audible artifact.

20 takes joined with 0.9-second pauses. Peaks limited (0.58), then loudness-normalized (linear, two-pass) to about -17 LUFS.
