Seventeen Voices, One Throat: The One Vehicle and the Open Sky, Part Seven
Released 22 November 2026 (revised recording, second release the same day).

Narrated by Tara using the Aoede AI voice. All 20 takes were machine-transcribed and compared with their actual take texts. A full base-model pass scored 72.6% to 98.6% word-sequence agreement per take. The three lowest takes were re-transcribed with a second, larger model (82.9% to 91.4%), and every span flagged as possibly missing was confirmed present in the audio by word-timestamped neighborhood re-transcription. The recurring differences are the recognizers' spellings of Sanskrit and Tibetan names; no substantive passage is missing. Final listening copy: 32:34, -17.1 LUFS integrated, -0.4 dBTP true peak, 128 kbps mono; complete decode verified. No human listened end to end, and no native-speaker pronunciation review was done. Transcription checks cannot certify every pronunciation or small audible artifact.

20 takes joined with 0.9-second pauses. Peaks limited (0.58), then loudness-normalized (linear, two-pass) to about -17 LUFS.
