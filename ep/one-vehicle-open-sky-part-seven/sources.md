# Source and accuracy ledger: Part 7, Seventeen Voices, One Throat
Compiled 22 November 2026. Every straight-quoted string in the frozen script was machine-checked against the staged source texts below (17 quotes, 0 misses; comparison normalizes case, whitespace, punctuation and diacritics). One quotation comes from the Part 6 staged text and was checked against it.

## What this episode is, and is not
The Seventeen Tantras themselves are NOT read in this episode. No free, verifiable English translation exists: the published English versions are copyrighted books, and the Tibetan originals survive as manuscripts that would need months of collation. Chapter 1 says this plainly on air. What the episode reads is a chorus of freely licensed translations of related Great Perfection texts, plus the tradition's own list of the Seventeen, labelled on air as the tradition's claim.

1. "The Tantra of Self-Emergent Awareness" (Pema Ledrel Tsal, Khandro Nyingtik cycle), translated 2025 with Tsadra Foundation support. https://www.lotsawahouse.org/tibetan-masters/pema-ledrel-tsal/self-emergent-awareness-tantra . The poem's practice imperatives are deliberately not read; the script says so.
3. Mipham Rinpoche, "The Vajra Mirror: Eliminating Doubts Concerning Self-Awareness," translated 2016. https://www.lotsawahouse.org/tibetan-masters/mipham/vajra-mirror-self-awareness . Quoted in chapter 5: "Ah! You who presume to follow the Middle Way"; "The dharmadhatu, which is beyond conception, is experienced individually and reflexively..."; "But here, self-awareness is great emptiness"; "Can you put your finger on it?"; the "many aeons of philosophical speculation" close.
4. Mipham Rinpoche, "A Dzogchen Instruction," translated 2026, dated in its colophon to 9 February 1898. https://www.lotsawahouse.org/tibetan-masters/mipham/dzogchen-instruction . Quoted in chapter 6: the opening claim; "spontaneously perfect within the ground"; "unobscured by the webs of conceptuality"; "perfect within a state of equality." Practice lines not read.
