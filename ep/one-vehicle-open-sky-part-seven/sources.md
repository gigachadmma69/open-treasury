# Part Seven, revision 2: source and accuracy ledger
22 November 2026. This revision supersedes the first script and audio. It corrects overstatements that exact-quotation checking did not detect.

## Scope and limits
The Seventeen Tantras themselves have not been read for this episode. I have not located and checked a complete English translation this course has permission to reproduce. That is a limit of this research, not proof no translation exists. The episode quotes and discusses four selected texts, not the contents of the Seventeen. The short-tantra chapter uses quotations and paraphrase, not a complete line-by-line recital. Comparisons with the Lotus are the course's interpretations, not claims that the traditions are identical.

## Primary translations consulted
All four are Adam Pearcey translations published by Lotsawa House, CC BY-NC 4.0. Quotations preserve wording, with verse line breaks joined and sentence-initial capitalization normalized. Sixteen straight-quoted strings checked against the staged source texts, zero misses.

- Pema Ledrel Tsal, The Tantra of Self-Emergent Awareness, translated 2025. https://www.lotsawahouse.org/tibetan-masters/pema-ledrel-tsal/self-emergent-awareness-tantra . A short text of the Khandro Nyingtik, not presented as one of the Seventeen. Chapter 3 quotes selected lines, discusses them, and paraphrases the close.
- Longchen Rabjam, The Heart of Definitive Meaning, translated 2025. https://www.lotsawahouse.org/tibetan-masters/longchen-rabjam/heart-definitive-zabmo-yangtik . Chapter 4 quotes awareness's beginningless purity, the space image and self-liberation. The course does not turn its practice imperatives into guided instructions.
- Mipham, The Vajra Mirror, translated 2016. https://www.lotsawahouse.org/tibetan-masters/mipham/vajra-mirror-self-awareness . Chapter 5 discusses a philosophical dispute about self-awareness and Madhyamaka. This does not establish the modern claim that practice is unnecessary or that ethics do not matter.
- Mipham, A Dzogchen Instruction, translated 2026. https://www.lotsawahouse.org/tibetan-masters/mipham/dzogchen-instruction . Chapter 6 quotes descriptive view passages. Its closing aspiration concerns both the practitioner's and others' welfare.

## Corpus descriptions, not readings of the corpus
- Rangjung Yeshe Wiki, Dzogchen tantras. https://rywikitexts.tsadra.org/index.php/Dzogchen_tantras . Used for a Vima Nyingtik list and variant counts, reported as the source's account. Its historical transmission narratives are lineage accounts, not independently dated events.
- Wisdom Publications transmission announcement. https://wisdomexperience.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Wisdom-Publications-17-Tantras-Transmission.pdf . Used for descriptions and analogies assigned to individual tantras in that announcement, not as evidence we have read their contents.

## Editorial checks still distinct from quotation checking
Matching quotations does not validate the interpretation, prove historical dating, establish completeness, clear rights outside the recorded licence, or certify narration. Audio must be checked against this revision's takes, with the actual extent of review described honestly. No practice instruction or transmission is offered. The open project of translating the Seventeen has no established timetable or completion commitment.
