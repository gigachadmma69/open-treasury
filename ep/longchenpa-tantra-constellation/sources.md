### Q1 Pearl Garland, four lines, chapter1, spoken chapter7
URL: https://rywikitexts.tsadra.org/index.php/Lung_gi_gter_mdzod/Chapter_1/
Marker: མུ་ཏིག་ཕྲེང་བ་ལས།
Tibetan: འདི་ལྟར་སྣ་ཚོགས་སྣང་བ་རྣམས། །ཐག་པ་ལ་ནི་སྦྲུལ་མཐོང་བཞིན། །དེ་མ་ཡིན་ལ་དེར་བཟུང་བས། །ཕྱི་ནང་སྣོད་བཅུད་གཉིས་སུ་ཆགས།
English: "These various appearances are like seeing a snake in a rope. By taking what is not that to be that, outer and inner, environment and inhabitants, become established as two."
Check: thagpa rope, sbrul snake; de mayin la der bzung misidentification; snodbcud container/contents conventionally environment/inhabitants; gnyis su chags twofold establishment, not automatically emotional attachment. The English does not add physical creation by a person's ego. Commentary immediately before concerns ignorance and grasping appearance as real; following prose extends dream/rope examples. Spoken explanation makes the limit explicit.

### Q2 Sixfold Expanse, first pair only, chapter10, spoken chapter11
URL: https://rywikitexts.tsadra.org/index.php/Lung_gi_gter_mdzod/Chapter_10/
Marker: ཀློང་དྲུག་པ་ལས།
Tibetan: རྙོག་པ་མེད་པའི་རང་སེམས་དང༌། །རྟོག་པ་མེད་པའི་རང་རིག་གཉིས། །འདྲའོ་འདྲའོ་ནོར་ར་རེ།
English: "One's unclouded mind and one's nonconceptual awareness: the two seem alike, alike. What a mistake!"
Check: rnyogpa muddiness/turbidity contrasted with rtogpa thought/conceptualization; rangsems and rangrig distinguished, not collapsed. nor ra re is idiomatic exclamation, not a literal word-for-word English phrase. Immediate prose explicitly compares clear/nonconceptual appearance while distinguishing subject-object fixation. Only first pair included; later subtle-body pairs excluded.

### Q3 Self-Liberated Awareness, first two lines only, chapter10, spoken chapter13
Same chapter10 URL.
Marker: རིག་པ་རང་གྲོལ་ལས།
Tibetan: ཡང་དག་ཤེས་པའི་ངོ་བོ་ལ། །བྱ་དང་བྱེད་པའི་རིག་པ་མེད།
English: "In the essence of authentic knowing, there is no awareness of deed and doer."
Check: yangdag shespa authentic knowing; ngobo essence; bya/byedpa action or what is done/agent. Do not replace them with an allegedly fixed subject/object pair. No supplied "divided into" in the final quote. The next line about grasping/deviation is discussed rather than quoted; causal morphology is not silently papered over. Surrounding prose distinguishes placement/nonplacement, then view/meditation/conduct/fruition; no subsequent attention instructions reproduced.

### Q4 and Q5 Reverberation of Sound, separate lines2 and4, chapter3, spoken chapter15
URL: https://rywikitexts.tsadra.org/index.php/Lung_gi_gter_mdzod/Chapter_3/
Marker: སྒྲ་ཐལ་འགྱུར་རྩ་བའི་རྒྱུད་ལས།
Q4 Tibetan: མཐའ་དང་བྲལ་བས་དབུས་མི་གནས།
English: "Free from extremes, it does not remain in a center."
Q5 Tibetan: ལྷག་མ་མེད་པས་ཆ་ཤས་མིན།
English: "With nothing left over, it is not a partial portion."
Check: mtha' extremes/limits; dbus center; lhagma remainder; chashas part/portion. These are explicitly two separate extracts, not a stitched continuous verse. Lines1/3 excluded: repetition of completion and the exact local force of bsngo are not declared settled. Context concerns uncontrived completeness, not scientific cosmology or an ordinary universal instruction to neglect duties.

### Q6 Samantabhadra's Mirror of Mind, one line, spoken chapter18
URL: https://rywikitexts.tsadra.org/index.php/Gnas_lugs_rin_po_che%27i_mdzod/Chapter_1/Commentary
Marker: ཀུན་ཏུ་བཟང་པོའི་ཐུགས་ཀྱི་མེ་ལོང་ལས།
Tibetan: སྣང་བ་སེམས་སུ་འདོད་པ་ང་ལས་གོལ་བ་ཡིན།
English: "To hold appearances to be mind is to stray from me."
Check: snangba appearances, sems mind; 'dodpa hold/assert, nga las golba deviation from me. Scriptural first person identified as such, not narrator. Immediate context explicitly distinguishes sems and byangchubkyisems, discusses cognition, potency, display and ornament, and rejects both simplistic identity and independent otherness. Adjacent Self-Arisen and Epitome citations are contextually discussed, not quoted in English. No independent full-root reading claimed.
