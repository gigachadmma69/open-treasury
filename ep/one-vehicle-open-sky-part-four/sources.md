### Chapter 3 (script): the testament, all quotes from the Pearcey Three Statements (2025), exact unless noted

1. "Homage to the confidence of realizing intrinsic awareness." Exact.
2. "This awareness, which has no concrete identity, is totally unconfined in its modes of manifestation. All that appears and exists thus arises as the dharmakaya field, and this very arising is liberated directly upon itself." Exact (diacritics removed).
3. "Introducing directly the face of rigpa itself. Deciding upon one thing and one thing only. Confidence directly in the liberation of rising thoughts." Exact.
4. "The mother recognizes the mother itself. The child recognizes itself." Exact.
5. "Determine that the apparent is one's own nature. Determine that your own experience is oneness. Determine that doubt is resolved in liberation." Exact.
6. "You decide that the stains will cleanse the stains, that thoughts will take care of themselves without having to employ some other remedy." Exact.
7. "Just as the snake itself uncoils its own knots." Exact.
8. "To have self-mastery brings such confidence as a rich person's wealth might afford. To have mastery over 'other' brings confidence like that of a universal emperor. To have mastery of the site of freedom brings confidence like space into which space dissolves." Exact.
9. "the tendency for awareness to be directed elsewhere fades away." Exact (embedded in the snake passage).
10. Testament frame: the fall of the casket into Manjushrimitra's hand, Sitavana, the river Danatika. Exact scene as in the source; paraphrased in the script.

### Chapter 4 (script)

11. "Encapsulating the wise intent of all the sugatas, this resuscitating instruction in three statements brings a decisive resolution of samsara and nirvana." Pearcey Three Statements. Note: the digitized text prints "ṇirvāṇa" (an OCR slip for nirvana); normalized.
12. "The Great Perfection of primordial freedom, which all sentient beings possess, does not need to be sought elsewhere." Jamyang Khyentse Chokyi Lodro, Spontaneously Written Song of Experience, tr. Pearcey 2020. Exact.
13. "To recognise this alone is sufficient." Same source. Exact.
14. "Simply to utter glib phrases, such as 'this is emptiness of which compassion is the very essence, so there is no need for anything else!' or 'not clinging at all is generosity,' is nothing more than delusion." Do-Ngak Chokyi Gyatso, Commentary on Some Terms from the Great Perfection, tr. Pearcey 2016. Compressed: the original lists three glib phrases; the script quotes two and drops "This is the power of awareness!"

### Chapter 5 (script): all quotes from Do-Ngak Chokyi Gyatso, tr. Pearcey 2016

15. Primordial purity definition: "Primordial purity is of two kinds. Firstly, it refers to the purity that is the absence of any true stains within the extremely subtle energy-mind that is present as the ground. Secondly, it refers to the fact that the thought-processes that develop out of the delusion of non-realization are not an integral part of our basic character, in the way that heat is a property of fire, but are by nature utterly pure." Exact; the parenthetical Tibetan gloss "(ka dag)" after the term is dropped in audio.
16. Spontaneous presence definition: "Spontaneous presence refers to the fact that when we apply the path to aspects of our energy-mind, the kayas and wisdoms arise merely through applying the key points, as these qualities, which have the character of fruitional enlightenment, have always been present intrinsically." Exact; the parenthetical gloss "(lhun grub)" is dropped in audio.
17. Ground-rigpa/path-rigpa: "In this Dharma tradition, the rigpa of the Great Perfection is a recognition in which one experiences the ground exactly as it is, and there is therefore no path-rigpa that is distinct from the ground-rigpa. Then again, nobody would claim that the mere indeterminate ground-rigpa, which is present once we have strayed from the actual condition of the ground, is the rigpa of the Great Perfection." Exact (lead-in "as my all-knowing guru asserted" dropped).
18. "Strayed from the ground"; appearances "manifest according to the fabrications of mind, while in reality mind itself is beyond arising and ceasing, and without basis or origin." Exact.

### Chapter 6 (script): the heart tantras

19. The catalog (seventeen tantras of the innermost unexcelled cycle; eighteen with the Ekajati protector tantra; nineteen in Padmakara's count; each complete in itself; "the full requirements for one person to practice and reveal complete buddhahood within a single lifetime"): summarized from the catalog reproduced from Tsele Natsok Rangdrol's Mirror of Mindfulness appendix (tr. Erik Pema Kunsang) at rywiki.tsadra.org/index.php/The_Dzogchen_Tantras. Factual summary, not verbatim quotation, except individual tantra titles and one-line roles (Dra Thalgyur "explains how to reveal the level of nirmanakaya and how to accomplish the welfare of others through practices related to sound").
20. Dra Talgyur title renderings (Reverberation of Sound; word-transcending; "primordial sound/resonance" gloss): Rigpa Wiki and scholarly summaries, cited for fact; the "reality is sound before it is anything else" line is original commentary and flagged as a thesis, not a translation.
21. All-Creating King: root tantra of the Mind Series (standard; also attested in Lotsawa House notes fetched today). Early-fragment scholarship: Dan Martin (2023), fragments at Matho, Ladakh, dating before c. 1200, no chapter divisions yet in the fragment, oldest witness apart from the Dunhuang Cuckoo of Awareness. Martin's phrase "puts forward the carrot of creationism only to pull the rug out from under the feet of foundationalism" is closely paraphrased in the script ("offers the carrot of a creator god, then pulls the rug out from under foundationalism") and attributed to him by name in the audio. The script's "your own awareness, accused, in the first person" is original commentary on the King's first-person voice.
