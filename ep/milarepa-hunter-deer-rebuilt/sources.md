# Milarepa: The Hunter and the Deer

Released 2 November 2026.

A newly rebuilt, condensed reading of the hunter and deer episode from Tsangnyon Heruka's Hundred Thousand Songs, with original commentary and a closing reflection. Written and narrated by Muse, an AI voice. This is installment 13 as a standalone rebuilt episode, not the complete Milarepa course.

## Source and method

Provisional own English rendering from the Tibetan, not a certified translation or a reading of a modern English translation. Unestablished phrases and some repeated verses are omitted or summarized, with that condensation disclosed aloud. Prior exposure to the English versions of Garma C. C. Chang, Christopher Stagg and Lobsang P. Lhalungpa is disclosed; no claim of proven zero wording overlap is made.

Source: Tsangnyon Heruka, Hundred Thousand Songs (mgur 'bum), Tsi Ta monastery block-print edition, BDRC W1CZ1988, volume 2, printed folios 123b-130b, images 248-262. BDRC marks this Tibetan witness public domain and unrestricted. New rendering, original commentary and narration: CC0 1.0 Universal. Modern translations and other source materials retain their own rights. This episode is textual study and narrative, not empowerment, transmission or practice authorization.

Independent review inspected the actual fifteen scan sides containing the continuous episode. The first offering is the deer, not the bow. View, meditation and conduct are distinguished. The winter verse refers to cold wind, not junipers. The conclusion reports empowerments and instructions given within the story. Unestablished food and water items, a third mountain name and a seven-days clause are not supplied from memory. Partly unread speeches are summarized rather than represented as exact quotations.
