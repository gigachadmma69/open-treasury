# Namkha Che: A Full 55-Verse Study Course

1 November 2026. Four units,55 verse sections,63:44.

A four-unit study course covering all fifty-five verses of Dorje Sempa Namkha Che in sequence. Original study paraphrases and commentary compare the short critical text with longer-tantra correspondences and selected published translations. Separate from the earlier interpretive companion.

Not a certified Tibetan translation or a reading of a modern translation. Difficult wording, witness variants and unresolved verses remain explicit. Traditional Garab Dorje attribution and Wilkinson's compilation hypothesis are distinguished. The longer thirty-three-chapter tantra is not treated as an identical witness. No restricted practice instructions or claims of attainment.

## Open questions

Especially verses 34,42-45,47,49-51 and 55 retain explicit limits about syntax, pronouns, line segmentation or differing witnesses. Where a full rendering is not secure, the course offers an attributed comparison or a cautious thematic explanation rather than an invented settled translation. Wilkinson's compilation proposal, including his discussion of 44-45, is identified as a hypothesis, not settled authorship.

No underlying source PDF is redistributed. Public accessibility is not presented as permission to narrate a copyrighted translation. Study paraphrases and explanations are our own prose, with brief attributed terms only. This is not an authorized lineage course.
