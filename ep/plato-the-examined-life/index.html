Episode 84

Plato: The Examined Life

24 October 2026 · 1:06:42

From Socrates's trial to the invisible ring, the divided soul, the cave and the philosopher-rulers: an introduction to Plato that takes the arguments seriously without excusing their political costs. Includes Diotima's account of love, the Phaedrus on writing, a source-honest translation note, and the myth of Er. History, dramatic presentation, myth and interpretation are kept distinct. Narrated by Tara, an AI voice. Provisional interpretation, not an authoritative translation, lineage transmission or practice authorization.

Full primary works read: the Apology and Crito in Benjamin Jowett's public-domain translation; the Republic, Symposium and Phaedrus appear as selected passages only, and the course says so - no whole-corpus claim. Socrates in the dialogues is a dramatic speaker, not a verbatim recording of the historical man. The oracle, the ring of Gyges, the cave, Diotima's account of Love's parentage, the Egyptian writing story and the myth of Er are attributed to their dramatic contexts, not treated as verified events. Moral and political criticism, present-day illustrations and the closing exercise are the course author's own interpretation and application. No long quotations; every argument is given in the course's own words. Original narration and commentary CC0 1.0 Universal.
