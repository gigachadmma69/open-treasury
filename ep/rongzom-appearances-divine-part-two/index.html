Reading Rongzom | Part Two

The World That Was Never Impure: Reading Rongzom, Part Two

A complete reading of Rongzom Chokyi Zangpo's Establishing Appearances as Divine, in Dominic Sur's translation, with selective commentary. The argument moves through primordial purity, reasoning and scriptural authority, the river seen as water or pus, shared perception, and the limits of even pure appearance. Direct scriptural references are explained for newcomers. Later tantric parallels are labelled comparisons, not invented citations. A study reading, not a new Tibetan translation, critical edition or empowerment.
