The Monk Who Wouldn't Behave: Ikkyu, Crazy Cloud, and the difference between freedom and an alibi

14 November 2026 | 36:21 | Nine chapters

A wooden sword, a book-burning monk, a sleeping musician, a sparrow's funeral, a city at war. Ikkyu is harder to turn into a permission slip when we read beyond the scandal. An original critical course about serious training, poetic play, grief and responsibility.

The difference between freedom and an alibi is this course's argument, not a newly discovered Ikkyu teaching. Surviving objects, traditional biography, poetry and our interpretation are kept distinct. Mori's feelings and consent are not invented. Irregular conduct is not a practice method or an exemption from care.
