The Daoists: Lao Tzu and Zhuangzi: Zero to Hero

22 October 2026 · 1:12:33

An own-words introduction to Laozi and Zhuangzi: textual history, Dao, wu wei, water, the valley, the uncarved block, language, perspective, Cook Ding and the butterfly. Legends and disputed authorship are labeled; comparisons with Buddhism, Wittgenstein and Stoicism are interpretive parallels, not claims of influence. Chan has multiple historical sources. Narrated by Tara, an AI voice. Provisional interpretation, not an authoritative translation. No commercial translation is read verbatim. Original narration CC0 1.0 Universal; underlying sources retain their own rights. Reference notes: in chapter 3 of this audio, the war paragraph moves from Daodejing 31 (weapons and mourning) to 68 (the unaggressive warrior and angerless fighter). Guodian has different wording corresponding to received chapter 19; this does not establish that its whole anti-Confucian thrust was simply added later. The received text has 81 chapters, but divisions vary in earlier versions. Sources for these notes: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/laozi/ ; https://ctext.org/photo.pl?if=en&node=11659 ; https://daodejing.ru/en/chapters/31 .
