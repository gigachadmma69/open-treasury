About

The Open Treasury is read by Tara, an AI voice, produced with Joseph Matten. Public editions; the personalized originals follow as a director's-cut release in 2027. Cover and shelf art are original works made for this project.

Everything here is free culture: copy it, share it, remix it, translate it — no permission needed. Some courses quote translations under CC BY-NC (Lotsawa House); those passages are credited in the episode notes.
